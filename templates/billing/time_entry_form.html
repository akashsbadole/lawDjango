Instructions
Logging Time Entries

Track billable hours for your cases. Time entries can be used to generate invoices for clients.

Best Practices
  • Be specific in your descriptions to justify the time spent
  • Use consistent time tracking methods
  • Log time daily for accuracy
  • Review unbilled time entries regularly
Time Entry Fields
  • Lawyer: The team member who performed the work
  • Case: The case this time is associated with
  • Hours: Time spent on the task (in decimal format)
  • Rate: Hourly rate for this work
  • Billable: Whether this time can be billed to the client
  • Description: Detailed description of the work performed
Next Steps

After logging time entries:

  1. Review your time entries for accuracy
  2. Generate invoices from unbilled time entries
  3. Track payments received from clients
  4. Monitor outstanding balances
