Instructions
Creating an Invoice

Fill in the form to create a new invoice for your client. You can add items to the invoice after it's created.

Invoice Status
  • Draft: Invoice is being prepared and not yet sent to client
  • Sent: Invoice has been sent to client and is awaiting payment
  • Paid: Invoice has been fully paid
  • Overdue: Invoice is past due date and still unpaid
  • Cancelled: Invoice has been cancelled
Tax Calculation

The tax rate should be entered as a decimal. For example:

  • 8% tax = 0.08
  • 7.5% tax = 0.075
  • 0% tax = 0.00
Next Steps

After creating the invoice:

  1. Add invoice items with descriptions, quantities, and rates
  2. Review the calculated totals
  3. Send the invoice to your client
  4. Record payments as they are received
