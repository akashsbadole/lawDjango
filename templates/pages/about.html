Our Story

Founded in 2018 by a team of legal professionals and technology experts, our journey began with a simple observation: too many talented lawyers were being held back by outdated, inefficient, and expensive practice management tools.

We set out to create a platform that would be powerful enough to handle the complex needs of modern law firms, yet simple and affordable enough for solo practitioners and small teams.

2018
Company founded by legal tech professionals with a vision to democratize legal practice management
2019

2021
