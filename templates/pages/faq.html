Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our law firm management platform

Getting Started

How do I get started with the platform? +

Getting started is easy! Simply sign up for a free trial, complete your firm profile, and follow our guided setup process. We'll walk you through importing your existing data, setting up your team, and configuring your preferences. Most firms are fully operational within 30 minutes.

Can I import my existing case data? +

Yes! We support importing data from various formats including CSV, Excel, and most major practice management systems. Our import wizard guides you through the process, and our support team is available to help with larger migrations.

Is there training available? +

We offer comprehensive training resources including video tutorials, documentation, and live webinars. Premium plans also include personalized onboarding sessions with our implementation specialists to ensure your team gets up to speed quickly.

Features

What features are included in each plan? +

Our plans are designed to grow with your practice:

  • Starter (Free): Basic case management, document storage, and client communication
  • Professional ($49/month): Advanced case management, billing, time tracking, and reporting
  • Enterprise ($99/month): All features plus advanced security, custom workflows, and priority support
Can I customize the platform for my practice area? +

Absolutely! Our platform is highly customizable. You can create custom fields, workflows, document templates, and reports tailored to your specific practice area. Enterprise plans include unlimited customizations and workflow automation.

Does the platform integrate with other software? +

Yes, we integrate with popular legal software including QuickBooks, Xero, Dropbox, Google Drive, Microsoft Office, and many more. We also offer API access for custom integrations on our Enterprise plan.

Pricing

Is there really a free plan? +

Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free forever and includes essential features for solo practitioners and small firms. There are no hidden fees or time limits, and you can upgrade at any time as your practice grows.

Can I change plans anytime? +

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll have immediate access to new features. When downgrading, changes take effect at the next billing cycle to ensure you don't lose access to important data.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), debit cards, and ACH bank transfers. For Enterprise plans, we also offer annual billing with additional discounts.

Security

How secure is my data? +

Security is our top priority. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption for all data transmission and storage, conduct regular security audits, and comply with industry standards including GDPR and HIPAA where applicable. Your data is backed up daily and stored in secure, redundant data centers.

Is the platform HIPAA compliant? +

Yes, our platform is HIPAA compliant for firms handling medical records or personal health information. We provide Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) and implement the necessary administrative, physical, and technical safeguards required by HIPAA.

Who owns my data? +

You own your data completely. We never sell, share, or use your data for any purpose other than providing the service. You can export your data at any time, and we guarantee data portability if you ever decide to leave the platform.

Technical

Do I need to install any software? +

No installation is required! Our platform is cloud-based and accessible from any web browser. We also offer mobile apps for iOS and Android, so you can work from anywhere, on any device.

What browsers are supported? +

We support all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, we recommend using the latest version of your preferred browser. Mobile browsers are also fully supported.

Is there an API available? +

Yes, we provide a comprehensive REST API for Enterprise customers. The API allows you to integrate with custom systems, build custom reports, and automate workflows. Full documentation and SDKs are available in our developer portal.

Billing

How does billing work? +

Billing is monthly or annual based on your chosen plan. You'll be charged automatically on your billing date, and you'll receive an email receipt for all charges. You can view your billing history and download invoices at any time from your account settings.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no penalties. If you cancel, you'll continue to have access to your plan until the end of your current billing period. We also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for new customers.

Do you offer refunds? +

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for all new subscriptions. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, contact our support team for a full refund. After 30 days, refunds are provided on a case-by-case basis.
