Data Protection Measures

We implement comprehensive data protection strategies to ensure your legal information remains secure, private, and available when you need it most.

Our data centers are protected by physical security measures including 24/7 monitoring, biometric access controls, and redundant power and cooling systems. We maintain multiple geographic regions for disaster recovery and business continuity.

All data is encrypted using industry-standard algorithms, and we maintain strict separation between customer data using multi-tenant architecture with logical isolation.
