Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing plans

Can I change my plan later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll have immediate access to all new features. When downgrading, you'll retain access to your current features until the end of your billing period.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), as well as PayPal and bank transfers for annual subscriptions.
Is there a setup fee?
No, there are no setup fees or hidden costs. The price you see is the price you pay, with no surprises.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. If you cancel, you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period, and you'll receive a prorated refund for any unused time.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or educational institutions?
Yes, we offer special pricing for qualifying non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Contact our sales team to learn more about our discounted rates.
