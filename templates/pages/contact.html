Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform and services

How do I get started with your platform?
Getting started is easy! Simply sign up for a free account, choose your subscription plan, and you'll be guided through a setup wizard to configure your firm's information, users, and preferences. Most firms are up and running within 30 minutes.
Can I import my existing case data?
Yes, we support data import from most major practice management systems. We offer CSV templates and automated import tools to help you migrate your existing case data, client information, and documents seamlessly to our platform.
What kind of support do you offer?
We offer comprehensive support including 24/7 email support, live chat during business hours, phone support for Professional and Enterprise plans, and an extensive knowledge base with video tutorials and documentation. Enterprise customers also get a dedicated account manager.
Is my data secure and compliant?
Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade security with 256-bit SSL encryption, regular security audits, and comply with industry standards including GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2. Your data is stored in secure, redundant data centers with regular backups.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. You'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period, and we'll provide options to export your data before cancellation.
